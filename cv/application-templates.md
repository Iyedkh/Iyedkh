## Tips

- **Personalize one sentence per company.** Mention something real: their product, a client, an e-commerce or SaaS focus.
- **Use the exact job title from the ad** in the subject line. Some companies filter by it.
- **Send from Gmail with a clean subject,** and attach the PDF named with your name (already done).
- **Log every application** in `job-tracker.csv` (Status, Date applied, Contact person) so you know when to follow up.
- **Don't send the same email to many companies in one batch** (no mass CC). Send each one separately.
